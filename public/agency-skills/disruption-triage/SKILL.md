# Route evidence review

## Question
Which current observations and agency statements concern this route?

## Method
Use the exact indexed route identity. Preserve feed-specific timestamps, unresolved trip counts, affected entities, and the agency alert text. A published alert is evidence that the agency issued the statement, not independent validation of its cause.

## Interpretation
Separate observed reports, predicted times, and published descriptions. No operational recommendation or causal claim is established by this collection alone. Save the evidence and record the next question in the notebook.
